What could go wrong, and what happens instead

Someone gets hold of your keys.

Press one button and your funds move to the wallets you picked in advance, before anyone else can take them.

Someone forces you to hand over access.

Your plan can’t be rewritten in a hurry. Every change waits 48 hours, and you can cancel it.

You lose access to your own wallet.

Add a backup wallet of yours to the plan. When the timer runs out, your funds go there.

You’re gone, and nobody can reach your crypto.

If you stop checking in, the people you chose receive their shares. No seed phrase changes hands.

You don’t want to trust a company with your money.

Nothing is locked and nobody holds it for you. Your funds stay in your account until the moment they move.

Peace of mind now. An impact that outlasts you.

One plan covers both: protection while you’re here, and a clear path for your crypto when you’re not.

While you’re here

  • Use your funds as you always do. Trading, swapping and withdrawing keep working.
  • Reminders arrive by email or Telegram, timed to your plan. One tap checks you in: no wallet, no fees.
  • If your keys are ever at risk, one button moves everything to safety.

When you’re gone

  • Everything goes where you want it to go: family, friends, or a cause you care about.
  • Each person receives their share in their own account, ready to withdraw to their own wallet.
  • Your crypto keeps doing good after you, instead of being lost forever.

Built so nobody has to be trusted

No one holds the key

The switch’s key is split across NEAR’s network of independent operators. The whole key never exists in one place.

The rules are public

An open contract decides when the key can be used, and your plan decides where funds go. Anyone can check both.

You stay in control

Change your plan, pause the switch or remove its permission whenever you want.
